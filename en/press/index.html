In one sentence

Visdap is a visual day planner that shows your whole day as colored sectors on a 24-hour dial: in the app, in Android widgets, on a Wear OS watch and on the Windows desktop.

Description

Visdap turns a day plan into a clock. Each task becomes a colored sector on a 24-hour dial, so sleep, work, study and family time fit on one circle. The hand points to the current moment, and the part of the day that has already gone can be darkened, which makes the time left easy to see.

On Android, the dial and the task list live in home screen widgets; on Wear OS, today's tasks appear on the watch with a tile and complications; on Windows, the dial sits on the desktop and can stay on top of other windows. Visdap needs no account and shows no ads. It is free to start, and a Pro subscription adds unlimited tasks, projects with deadlines, recurring tasks and Pomodoro.

Key features

  • 24-hour clock

    The whole day on one dial, with no AM/PM confusion. Round or square, Arabic or Roman numerals.

  • Widgets

    A 24-hour clock and a task list on your Android home screen; on Windows the dial sits on your desktop.

  • Reminders you hear

    Loud reminders with sound and snooze, right on time.

  • Plan your days

    A month and year calendar, priorities S/A/B/C and checklists for every task.

  • Projects and deadlines

    Up to 20 projects. Deadline stripes grow on the dial every day, up to 4 at once.

  • Pomodoro with a log

    25 minutes of focus, then a break. Each session is credited to a project with a short note.
