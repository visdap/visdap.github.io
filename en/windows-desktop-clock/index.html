Windows

A 24-hour clock on your Windows desktop

Visdap keeps your whole day on a round 24-hour dial in the corner of the screen, so you see how much of a work block is left without switching windows.

  • No account
  • No ads or tracking
  • Works offline

What you get on Windows

  • Always in sight

    The dial can stay on top of other windows. In ghost mode, clicks pass through it to the window below.

  • Your size and transparency

    Any size from 150 to 900 pixels, with adjustable transparency.

  • Quick capture

    Press Ctrl+Shift+Space to add a task from any app.

  • Reminders, Pomodoro and projects

    Reminders with sound, a Pomodoro timer and projects with deadline stripes. Pomodoro and projects come with Pro.

Questions and answers

Which Windows versions are supported?

Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) and Windows 11.

Can it start with Windows?

Yes. You can turn on start with Windows in the settings.

Does it need internet?

No. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection to the Microsoft Store.
