Notes on planning a real day

Short, practical ideas about time. Most of them work with a pencil and a circle, with or without our app.

  • How many times were you interrupted today?

    Say a question takes five minutes. Finding your place again can take about as long, and nobody counts that part.

  • How many hours did you not bill last week?

    Emails before the project starts, estimates, one more small edit, invoices. It doesn't feel like work, and it still takes your evening.

  • What a month calendar can't show you

    A month page tells you which days are taken. It can't tell you how full they are.

  • The longest gap between your meetings

    A week grid makes thirty-minute meetings look small. The problem is what's left between them.

  • Float: a project management idea your day is missing

    Project schedules have float. Your own day usually doesn't.

  • Rolling wave planning for your study plan

    Project managers use it on real projects, and it works just as well when you study for the PMP® exam.

  • Whose day is this? Three puzzles

    No task names, only the shape. These three days are made up. Can you guess who each one belongs to?

Visdap shows your day as colored sectors on a 24-hour dial: on your Windows desktop, on your Android phone, and on your Wear OS watch. Free plan: 2 tasks a day (Android: up to 7 days ahead; Windows: today only). Pro is a paid subscription. Windows and Android plans don't sync.
