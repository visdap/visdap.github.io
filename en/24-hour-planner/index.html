Visdap › To-do list vs. 24-hour circle

Visual day planner

A to-do list shows what. A 24-hour circle shows when.

A list tells you what to do but hides the clock. Visdap draws your whole day as colored sectors around a 24-hour dial, so you see how long each task takes, where the free gaps are and how much of today is left.

  • No account
  • No ads or tracking
  • Works offline

List or circle: what changes

  • Time takes up space

    In a list, a ten-minute call and a three-hour block look the same. On the circle, the longer the task, the longer its arc.

  • Free gaps are visible

    Stretches of the ring with no sector are time nothing is planned for yet. You see them before you overload the day.

  • You see “now”

    The hand points at the current moment, and the part of the day that has passed can be shaded, so what's left is obvious at a glance.

  • All 24 hours, sleep included

    Morning, work, evening and sleep on one dial, with no AM/PM mix-ups. On Android, the same day shows in the app, in the widget and on the watch. The Windows app keeps its own plan.

Questions and answers

Is Visdap a to-do list?

It's a day planner. Each task gets a time, a color and, if you like, a reminder and a checklist. Instead of a long list, tasks become sectors on a 24-hour dial.

Do I have to plan every minute?

No. Add only what matters; the rest of the ring stays free time. The free version allows 2 tasks a day, and Pro removes the limit.

Which devices does it work on?

Android phones, Wear OS watches and Windows PCs. iPhone and Mac versions are on the way.
