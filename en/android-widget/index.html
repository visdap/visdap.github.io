Android

A 24-hour clock widget for your home screen

Visdap puts your whole day on a round 24-hour clock right on the Android home screen. Your tasks appear as colored sectors around the dial.

  • No account
  • No ads or tracking
  • Works offline

What you get on Android

  • Clock widget

    A round 24-hour clock with your tasks as sectors, so you see the day without opening the app.

  • Task list widget

    Today's tasks with their times in a separate widget.

  • Floating clock

    A small 24-hour clock on top of other apps, if you allow it.

  • Live wallpaper

    The 24-hour clock as a live wallpaper on your home and lock screens.

Questions and answers

Are the widgets free?

Yes. Both widgets are part of the free version. Pro adds unlimited tasks, projects on the dial and more.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer.

Do the widgets need internet?

No. They show the tasks stored on your phone.
