Read your day like a clock

It's a clock face stretched over all 24 hours. After a minute it reads itself.

  1. Midnight is at the top, noon at the bottom.
  2. The hand points to right now.
  3. Each color is one task.
  4. The longer the arc, the longer it takes.
  5. Dark teal gaps are free time.
  6. Darkened is the part of today that's already gone.

How it works

  1. Add your tasks

    Give each task a time, a color and, if you like, a reminder.

  2. See your day

    Tasks appear as sectors on a 24-hour clock: in the app, in a widget and on your watch.

  3. Stay on time

    Reminders with sound and snooze tell you when it's time to switch.

Questions and answers

Do I need an account?

No. Visdap works without sign-up, and your plans are stored on your device.

How is Visdap different from my calendar?

A calendar lists events. Visdap draws the whole day as a clock, so you see time passing and the free gaps between tasks. On Android it can show your calendar events next to your tasks, read-only, and it never changes them.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Planning, reminders and widgets work offline. Only buying or restoring Pro needs a connection to your store.

Is there a version for iPhone or Mac?

Both are on the way. For now Visdap is available for Android phones, Wear OS watches and Windows.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited tasks, projects on the dial, recurring tasks, planning on any date, Pomodoro, all clock styles including Luxury and your choice of reminder sound. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription; the price is shown in your store.

What happens to my plans if Pro ends?

They stay. Nothing you created is deleted; the Free limits apply only when you add new tasks.

Are there ads?

No. Neither the free version nor Pro shows ads.

Is Visdap a medical app?

No. Visdap helps you plan and see your time. It is not a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat anything.

Which languages does Visdap speak?

English, Ukrainian, German, Spanish and French.
